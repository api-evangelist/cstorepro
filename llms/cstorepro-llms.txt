# PDI Essentials

> PDI Essentials (formerly CStore Essentials / Skupos) is a software platform for convenience store owners and operators. It provides back office management, scan data reporting, tobacco loyalty, GasBuddy integration, and financial management tools — purpose-built for independent and chain c-stores.

## Solutions

- [Back Office & Operations](https://www.pdiessentials.com/solutions/back-office/): Real-time c-store operations — sales, pricebook, inventory, lottery, fuel, and automated reporting.
- [Skupos Scan Data](https://www.pdiessentials.com/solutions/scan-data/): Automated scan data submission and monthly cash-back payments from tobacco manufacturers.
- [Skupos Engage](https://www.pdiessentials.com/solutions/engage/): Tobacco loyalty and brand-funded promotions for independent c-stores.
- [GasBuddy Business Pages](https://www.pdiessentials.com/solutions/gasbuddy/): Reach 50 million monthly active drivers — monitor foot traffic and promote your station.
- [Financial Management](https://www.pdiessentials.com/solutions/financial/): Automated financial reporting, reconciliation, timesheet management, and account visibility.
